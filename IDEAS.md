# Skill ideas

Subjects I want skills for, kept as a list of topics rather than designs. A
line here is a standing itch, not a commitment: it says the area is worth a
skill, and says nothing yet about what the skill does or whether it belongs in
a **promoted** bucket.

Keep entries at the level of a subject. The moment an entry has a process, a
name, and a shape, it has outgrown this file: work it up with `/grill-me`,
write it with `/writing-for-agents`, and delete the line.
